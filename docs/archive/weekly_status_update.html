Rules and templates, runs in the browser, sends nothing anywhere. Median 0.05 s.
Local model mode (research)
qwen2.5:3b helps plan and write; llava-phi3 reads photos. Both free, on the laptop through Ollama. No Claude or GPT.

Seconds per answer (median): the model is 100 to 400 times slower

Honesty: says "unknown" when it should

Rules-only ran on 174 questions; the model variants on a sample of 44 (8 for retrieval), so those percentages are rough. The answer key was written by the developer and has not been checked by others yet. Finding: the small model did not beat plain rules, so rules ship.

Photo questions
People cannot always describe a sign, flyer or entrance, and blind or low-vision users need another way to ask. The photo is shrunk and redrawn in the browser (GPS removed), described by a local vision model, and never stored. The model is not allowed to say a place is accessible. Current limit: it needs a local model, and answer quality has been tested with a stub, not yet with the real model.

Privacy rules in place
Public pages only, no logins or CAPTCHAs. Personal emails become [email removed]; cell, home and personal numbers removed; raw pages never published; we publish facts with short quotes and links. Opt-out page, and a site that asks is removed and remembered. Reddit only through approved API.
Open items
Terms check only finds terms at usual addresses, in English. Oxford business sites and DC and Dayton government sites were crawled before the terms check existed. No named contact for site owners yet. Counsel and IRB review open.
